Clinical Safety Protocol

The Blood Donation Journey

Donating blood takes under an hour from start to finish, while the actual blood draw takes only 8-10 minutes.

1

Quick Registration

Scan your digital donor pass or present photo identification. Fill a brief confidential lifestyle questionnaire on the tablet.

2

Health Screening

A trained nurse checks your temperature, pulse, blood pressure, and tests a small droplet for safe hemoglobin levels (≥ 12.5 g/dL).

3

The Donation

Relax in a comfortable ergonomic recliner. Sterile, single-use equipment collects roughly 450 mL of whole blood in approximately 10 minutes.

4

Recovery & Refreshment

Enjoy light refreshments, juice, and rest for 15 minutes. Receive an instant update when your unit is dispatched to save a patient.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about blood donation eligibility, clinical safety protocols, and how BloodLink coordinates emergency requests.

Most healthy individuals between 18 and 65 years old weighing at least 50 kg (110 lbs) with a hemoglobin level of ≥ 12.5 g/dL can donate. You should not have active infectious diseases, recent tattoos or piercings within the last 3-6 months depending on jurisdiction, or untreated hypertension.
BloodLink cross-checks the international ABO and Rh(D) antigen rules before matching requests. For instance, O- units are prioritized for acute trauma where cross-matching isn't completed yet, while AB+ recipients are safely matched across all compatible donor pools. Cross-match verification at the receiving blood bank remains mandatory before physical transfusion.
Emergency Code-Red beacons require hospital confirmation or authorized doctor credentials. Public emergency requests undergo algorithmic verification and telephone triage before notification broadcasts are dispatched to registered donors.
Whole blood donation requires a minimum waiting period of 56 days (8 weeks) for men and 84 days (12 weeks) for women to allow the bone marrow to replenish red blood cells and iron stores. Platelet donations can be made more frequently (every 7 to 14 days).
